\## Introduction to My Computer Science ePortfolio



My ePortfolio represents the culmination of my computer science education and demonstrates how I have applied the skills developed throughout the program.



For my capstone, I selected Travlr Getaways, a full-stack travel application originally developed during CS 465. I chose this artifact because it provided an opportunity to demonstrate several areas of computer science within a single application.



Rather than presenting three unrelated projects, I wanted to show how software engineering, algorithms, data structures, and databases work together to create a more complete and effective system.



The portfolio is organized around three enhancements, each addressing a different area of computer science.



\*\*Software Design and Engineering\*\*



The first enhancement focused on improving the application's architecture and expanding its functionality. I developed a dynamic travel listing page and implemented a reservation workflow that includes customer input, validation, pricing calculations, and confirmation processing.



This enhancement demonstrates my ability to work with an existing application, evaluate its structure, and introduce new functionality while maintaining separation of concerns and considering the user experience.



\*\*Algorithms and Data Structures\*\*



The second enhancement focused on improving how travel information is organized and retrieved. I implemented a Map data structure for trip-code lookups and introduced searching, filtering, and sorting functionality.



This enhancement demonstrates my ability to apply algorithmic thinking and select data structures that support application requirements while improving the way users interact with information.



\*\*Databases\*\*



The third enhancement focused on persistent data management. I integrated MongoDB and Mongoose, created a reservation schema, implemented database validation and indexing, and developed functionality for saving and retrieving reservation information.



I also added reservation history pagination and aggregation reporting to demonstrate how stored data can be organized and analyzed.



This enhancement highlights my database development skills and connects closely with my professional experience in analytics and reporting.



Together, these three enhancements demonstrate how different areas of computer science support one another. The software engineering enhancement provides the application's functionality and structure, the algorithms and data structures enhancement improves how information is processed and retrieved, and the database enhancement ensures that reservation information can be stored and analyzed.



The project also required me to apply testing, debugging, documentation, version control, and security practices across all three areas. By the completion of development, the application had passed nine final regression checks, and the final dependency security audit reported zero vulnerabilities.
